Team talk

After each clue, tell your group: “I chose ___ because ___ begins with the /___/ sound.” Take turns being the picture pointer, sound speaker and letter chooser.

Creator challenge: Choose another classroom object that begins with s, a, t or p. Draw it on paper and write its first letter. Explain your choice to your group.
